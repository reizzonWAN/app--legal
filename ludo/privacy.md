Ludo ("the app") is a game developed by Reizon Games ("we", "us"). This policy explains how the app handles your
information on Android and iOS.

> **In short:** the app collects no personal data. Your games, settings and statistics stay on your device.

## What we collect

Nothing. The app does not collect, send, sell or share any personal data.

- No account or sign-in is needed.
- The app contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking software.
- The app makes no network requests while you play.

## What stays on your device

The app keeps these on your device so you can carry on where you left off:

- your settings: sound, haptics, game speed, house rules and display name;
- your game statistics;
- a game in progress, so you can resume it.

This data never leaves your device through the app. Your phone's own backup, such as Android backup to your Google
account or an iCloud backup, may include it, under the terms of that service. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

## Other services the app opens

The app itself never connects to the internet. A few actions hand you over to other apps, which handle your
information under their own privacy policies:

- **Rate Ludo**, and the occasional rating prompt after a win, are provided by Google Play or the App Store.
- **Contact support** opens your own email app with our address filled in. We see only what you choose to send.
- **Privacy policy** opens this page in your browser.

## Permissions

The app asks for no runtime permissions. It uses vibration for haptics and plays audio. It doesn't use the
microphone, camera, location, contacts or storage.

## Children

The app is suitable for all ages. It doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children
under 13.

## Your choices

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You can remove everything
the app keeps by uninstalling it, or by clearing the app's storage in your phone's settings.
